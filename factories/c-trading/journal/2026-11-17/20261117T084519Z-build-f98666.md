## Why killed instead of fixed
- The engine applies its own no-trade band of 0.02 to each coin separately (`engine/config.yaml` line 31, `engine/backtest.py` `_band`, lines 31-41; I read both). With the exposure split across 10 coins, a 0.02 band per coin equals a 0.20 band on total exposure.
- The card's `band` range is 0.10 to 0.30, so below 0.20 the engine overrides it. Positions at a total exposure of 0.20 or less can also never open from cash.
- A sweep would show a flat region from 0.10 to 0.20. That flatness would come from the engine, not from a stable idea, and every one of those trials would still count against the bar.
- No code change fixes this without changing the card's rules (equal split, band range). Both the skill and hard rule 5 forbid that. A second review of the same code would reject it again.
- I judged this to be a card that does not fit the engine, not an engine fault, so I did not raise `F-ENG-1`. The per-coin band is a reasonable cost guard.
