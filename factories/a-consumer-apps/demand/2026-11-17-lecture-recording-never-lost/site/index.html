Concept · In development · Early access

Record the whole lecture. Never lose it.

LectureSafe is a lecture recorder for your iPhone. It saves your audio to the phone in short pieces while you record, and brings back an interrupted recording the next time you open it.

  • Saves your recording as you record.
  • Recovers an interrupted recording on next launch.
  • Shows how much is saved while you record, and checks storage before a long lecture.

This app is in development. It is not on the App Store yet, and the screens in our ads are concept art, not the app.
